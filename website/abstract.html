Abstract

Traffic signs tell drivers about speed limits, hazards and the rules of the road, and recognising them automatically is an important part of driver-assistance and self-driving systems. This project builds a deep learning system that recognises traffic signs in images.

We trained a convolutional neural network (CNN) on the German Traffic Sign Recognition Benchmark (GTSRB), which has more than 50,000 real-world photos of 43 kinds of traffic sign taken in different lighting, weather, distances and angles. Each image is resized to 30x30 pixels and passed through four convolutional layers, two max-pooling layers, dropout and two fully connected layers, ending in a softmax output over the 43 sign classes.

The trained model correctly recognises 95.1% of the 12,630 images in the separate GTSRB test set. It can be tried through this website and through a desktop application: upload a picture of a traffic sign, and the system names the sign and shows how confident it is.
